## Course Structure

**In-Class Learning** — Weekly lectures and live demos covering:
- **Excel & Google Sheets**: Formulas, data cleaning, pivot tables, dashboards
- **SQL**: Querying, data manipulation, advanced techniques
- **BI Tools**: Dashboards & reports in Looker, Tableau, & Domo 
- **AI Fundamentals**: How AI/LLMs work, prompting, leveraging in data analytics, & hand-ons challenges 

**Weekly Work** — Each week includes a required **data assignment** plus an **optional AI challenge** to go deeper.

**Leveraging AI** — Use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and/or other tools as your study buddy and data tutor for explanations, feedback, and assignment support.

**GitHub** — Fork this repo, track your progress in each Module README (check off tasks weekly), and sync your fork regularly:

## 🤖 AI as a Tutor

AI is absolutely encouraged — it's an important part of today's data careers.

- **Use AI as a teacher, not a shortcut.** Ask it to explain concepts, troubleshoot errors, walk through problems, or quiz you — not just generate something to copy and paste.
- **Understand it. Verify it. Make it your own.** AI can be wrong; evaluating its output is part of becoming a strong data professional.
- **Cite your AI use** on assignments, projects, analysis, or code — use it openly, not secretly. E.g., *"I used ChatGPT to help troubleshoot my SQL query and explain the error."*
