## 📋 Class Agenda — Saturday, August 29th, 2026

### 🕐 Hour 1: Discussion, Housekeeping, Lecture & Activity

- **👋 Group Discussion & Check-In** (10 min)
  - Quick round-robin: how's everyone's week going with the material so far?

- **📌 Housekeeping** (5 min)
  - Announcements, upcoming deadlines, reminders (GitHub repo, next session's topic)
  - Q&A on logistics

- **📊 Lecture: Data Foundations** (25 min)
  - How to read a chart (title, axes, legend, data labels, scale, source)
  - Types of charts — bar, line, pie, scatter
  - Data roles in tech — Data Analyst, Data Engineer, Analytics Engineer, BI Analyst
  - Statistics basics — mean, median, mode, range (Bill Gates walks into a bar 🍺)

- **💻 Zoom Breakout Activity: "AI Spending Analyst"** (20 min)
  - Break students into small groups (breakout rooms, 3–4 per room)
  - Give each group a sample spending dataset (a simple CSV or list of transactions — e.g. a month of mock credit card charges by category)
  - **✏️ Step 1 — By hand:** group manually calculates mean, median, and mode of the spending amounts
  - **🤖 Step 2 — With AI:** group pastes the same data into ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini and asks it to calculate mean, median, mode, and analyze spending patterns
  - As a team, they answer using the chat or a shared Google Doc:
    1. Did your manual calculations match the AI's?
    2. What categories is the AI identifying, and does that match what you'd expect?
    3. What trend or insight did the AI surface (biggest category, unusual spike, etc.)?
    4. Would you trust this analysis? What would you double-check yourself?
  - Reconvene in main room: each group shares out for 1–2 min 🎤
